Claude web search

The catch-all: searches the live web and answers with sources. Covers what no database has, such as associations, language exchanges and local events.

France Travail job offers

Official French job offers. Lyon: commune of Lyon plus 10 km, or the Rhône département if the commune filter is refused.

France Travail agency for a postal code

The user types the postal code they know, such as 69001 to 69009 for Lyon. It is converted to the INSEE code in the background, and the agency is found.

France Travail explorer

Test any France Travail API added to your application. The presets are starting points from memory and may be out of date: open the API's documentation on francetravail.io (eye icon), compare the scope and the path, and correct them here before running. Needs the secret FT_EXPLORER=1.

Adzuna job offers

Job offers aggregated from many sites, with salaries. Lyon: within 20 km.

Social benefits estimate (OpenFisca)

Estimates RSA, prime d'activité, family allowances and, for Lyon, housing aid. Estimates only; the CAF decides the real amount.

Useful places nearby (OpenStreetMap)

Libraries, community centres, social services, job agencies, language schools, health centres. France-wide needs a city name.

Places near the user (map)

Type the user's postal code or address, or use your location. Places in the radius are sorted by distance: the 10 nearest are numbered in blue, the others in grey. "Directions" opens the route in Google Maps.

    Country comparison (World Bank)

    National indicators, so Lyon uses France. Compares France with countries a user may move to.
