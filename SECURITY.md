# Security Policy

APA (AI Personal Assistant) is a project of Nationsorg, a non-profit association. The safety and privacy of our users come first. Thank you for helping us keep APA secure.

## Official website

The only official APA website is:

https://apaworld.org

Any other address using the APA World name or logo is not ours. If you find a copy of APA pretending to be the official service, please report it to us.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please do not report security problems in public GitHub issues, discussions or pull requests.**

Report them privately, in one of these ways:

1. **GitHub private report:** open the **Security** tab of this repository and click **Report a vulnerability**
2. **Email:** security@apaworld.org

Please include, if possible:

- a short description of the problem and its possible impact
- the steps to reproduce it
- the page, file or function concerned

You can write in English.

## What happens next

- We will confirm that we received your report as soon as possible.
- We will keep you informed while we investigate and fix the problem.
- Once it is fixed, we will gladly credit you, if you wish.

## Please do

- Test only with your own account and your own data.
- Stop and report as soon as you find a problem.
- Give us reasonable time to fix it before talking about it publicly.

## Please do not

- Access, change or delete other users' data.
- Run attacks that could slow down or stop the service (for example denial of service or mass automated requests).
- Use social engineering or phishing against our users or team.
- Test our third-party providers (Supabase, GitHub, Twilio and others) directly. Report problems with their services to them.

## Personal data

For questions about personal data and privacy (GDPR), please contact our data protection contact: dpo@apaworld.org
